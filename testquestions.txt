1 Simple factual (single-claim)
“When was JFK assassinated?”
“What is the capital of Canada?”
“Who is the CEO of NVIDIA in 2026?”
2 Multi-part factual
“What is LangGraph, who maintains it, and when was it first released?”
“Compare Python and Go release years, creators, and primary use cases.”
“Who founded OpenAI, when, and where is its headquarters?”
3 Time-sensitive / dynamic web
“What are the latest major updates in Ollama this month?”
“What is the current Bitcoin price and 24h change?”
“What is the latest NASA mission news this week?”
4 Conflicting / controversial claims
“Did coffee increase lifespan according to recent studies?”
“Is intermittent fasting better than calorie counting for weight loss?”
“Are e-cigarettes safer than regular cigarettes?”
5 Local event + recency
“What happened in Turkey’s economy this week according to major sources?”
“Latest developments about EU AI Act enforcement timeline.”
“Recent major earthquake reports in the Mediterranean region.”
6 Numeric/statistical claims
“What is the global population now?”
“What is Türkiye’s inflation rate this year?”
“How many users does ChatGPT have currently?”
7 Entity disambiguation
“Tell me about Apple’s latest quarterly results.” (company, not fruit)
“What is Jaguar’s newest model?” (car brand ambiguity)
“Who is Jordan?” (person/country ambiguity)
8 Source reliability stress tests
“Did [viral social media claim] happen? verify with reliable sources.”
“Is ‘X supplement cures cancer’ true?”
“Fact-check: ‘The Earth’s core has stopped spinning.’”
9 Deep-reading required
“Summarize key points from this page and cite them: [URL]”
“What does this official announcement say about deadlines? [URL]”
“Extract 3 factual claims from this report page: [URL]”
10 Negative / no-evidence tests
“Did Einstein publish a paper in 2020?”
“Which country elected Elon Musk as president?”
“Did NASA confirm life on Mars yesterday?”
